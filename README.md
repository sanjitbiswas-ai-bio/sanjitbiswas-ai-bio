# Sanjit Biswas

Biotechnology researcher working in **microbial genomics, antimicrobial resistance, bacterial pathogenesis, and computational biology**.

My research interests focus on understanding bacterial pathogens through genomic, microbiological, and bioinformatic approaches, with particular interest in *Helicobacter pylori*, antimicrobial resistance, virulence, and microbial evolution.

## Research Interests

- Microbial genomics
- Antimicrobial resistance
- Bacterial pathogenesis and virulence
- Comparative genomics
- Phylogenetics and population genomics
- Biological sequence analysis
- Metagenomics
- Reproducible bioinformatics
- AI-assisted biological data analysis

## Computational Biology

**Programming:** Python · R · Bash

**Genomics:** sequence analysis · comparative genomomics · genome annotation · phylogenetic analysis · AMR analysis

**Data Analysis:** pandas · NumPy · SciPy · statistical analysis · scientific visualization

**Research Workflows:** reproducible analysis · automated testing · biological data processing

## Current Projects

### Bioinformatics Agent Benchmark

A Python-based scientific benchmark for evaluating computational agents on bioinformatics problems involving FASTA sequence analysis, biological sequence processing, automated validation, and reproducible testing.

### Microbial Genomics

Development of computational workflows for studying bacterial genomes, virulence-associated genes, antimicrobial-resistance determinants, genomic variation, and evolutionary relationships.

### Antimicrobial Resistance

Analysis of antimicrobial susceptibility, MIC distributions, multidrug-resistance patterns, resistance-associated genomic variation, and genotype–phenotype relationships.

## Organisms of Interest

*Helicobacter pylori* and other bacterial pathogens relevant to antimicrobial resistance, microbial evolution, and host-associated disease.

## Scientific Focus

My goal is to integrate **experimental microbiology, genomics, bioinformatics, and computational approaches** to investigate microbial evolution, pathogenicity, and antimicrobial resistance.
